*** Pixel Realm commands ***

/refresh - Refreshes whole realm (files and assets).
/refreshdir - Refreshes files in realm.
/refreshassets - Refreshes realm assets.
/tp [x] [y] [z] [dir] - Teleports player. If no parameters are provided, player will be teleported to (1000, 0, 1000).
/regeneratetrees - Regenerates trees in the realm.
/goto <path> - Go directly to specified path.
/fov [x] [y] - Sets field of view. If no parameters are provided, default values are used. Does not save changes on restart.
/unstuck - Moves out-of-bound items back into realm terrain.
/upgrade - Upgrades old realms to newest V2 version.
/acam - Sets controlling camera to actual camera (what is actually being rendered).
/vcam - Sets controlling camera to virtual camera (free roaming from player).
/pcam - Sets controlling camera back to player.
/templates - Shows the realm templates menu.
/tutorial - Starts the tutorial
/playerpos - Shows/hides player position.
/editgui - Allow editing of the GUI.
/touchcontrols - Shows touch controls.
/sensitivity <val> - Sets the turn sensitivity with touch controls.
